TRANSPARENT SCIENCE

Know the source.

Every good scientific atlas distinguishes observations, interpolations and artistic interpretations.

01

Measured

Depths recorded with instruments such as multibeam sonar. Site-specific published values are shown with source links when available.

DIRECT OBSERVATION
02

Interpolated

GEBCO's global grid combines direct soundings with estimates and interpolation where measurements are scarce. Its shaded relief is not an image taken by a satellite of the actual ocean floor.

MODELLED BATHYMETRY
03

Illustrated

Animated dives, water color, lights and 3D globe effects are representations for education. They are not underwater cameras or measured local conditions.
